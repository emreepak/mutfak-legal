3. Scan photos

Photos are resized on your phone and their metadata (EXIF, including location) is removed. They are uploaded to private storage only you can access and deleted once the ingredient recognition finishes, and in any case automatically within 24 hours.

5. How long we keep data

  • Scan photos: deleted after processing, at the latest within 24 hours.
  • Account, pantry, list, favorite and preference data: until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, all of the above is permanently deleted. The stores' own purchase records are governed by Apple's and Google's policies.

6. Security

Each user's data is isolated with row-level security in our database, so no other user can see it. Connections are encrypted. AI and payment keys are kept on our server only.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state privacy laws in the US), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Email emre@yunusemrepak.com from your account email and we will reply within 30 days. You can also delete your account in the app (how?).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children. Users under 18 should use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

9. Changes

When we update this policy we change the date above and announce material changes in the app.
